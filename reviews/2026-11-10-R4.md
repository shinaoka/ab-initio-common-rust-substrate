- **Translation fidelity.** The English and Japanese files are line-aligned (same line count except `esm-field-corehole.md`, offset by 3 lines).
  Numbers were compared line by line; all differences are Japanese counter words ("2 反復目" -> "second iteration", "3 つ" -> "three", ...)
